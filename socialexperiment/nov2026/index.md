**[Social Experiment](/socialexperiment)** is a free and informal, semi-regular evening gathering at Contact for anyone interested in Live Art and contemporary performance to meet, chat, and try ideas out…         
        
If you're a North West artist — this is your <a href="https://socialexperiment.posthaven.com" target="_blank">chance to test out</a> a short performance, intervention, or durational work, in front of an informal audience.         
         
If you're an audience member looking for something a little bit different — this is your chance to take a few risks & meet some artists in our social experiment.

An interactive, intimate piece that explores childhood memory, familial traditions, pop culture references, the temperance movement, Mancunian heritage, a global brand, purple stains, and facial hair.<br>Come and create your own Vimto Moustache.

**PHØBIA** · *Untitled (Face Yourself)*        
         
How many faces can one person have?<br>In *Face Yourself*, genderless drag creature PHØBIA creates a face, performs in it, photographs it, wipes it away, and begins again.<br>Across three hours, an archive of discarded identities slowly accumulates. Each photograph preserves a version of the performer that no longer exists.<br>Part endurance performance, part live self-portrait, *Face Yourself* invites audiences to witness the labour, vulnerability, and endless possibilities of transformation.<br>Come and go as you please. The face you leave behind might not be the one you return to.         
         
Access: a durational performance, running throughout the evening; come and go.        
         
PHØBIA is a Manchester-based genderless drag creature and performance artist working across horror drag, autobiographical theatre, and durational Live Art. A Factory International Fellow, their practice combines transformation, endurance, absurdity and vulnerability — challenging expectations of what drag can be. Their work includes *I Am Sixteen and Stupid*, a four-hour durational performance premiered at Emergency 26 for Word of Warning + Contact.         
         
Insta <a href="https://instagram.com/thatcreaturephobia" target="_blank">@thatcreaturephobia</a>

**Chanje Kunda** · *The Grace Card*         
         
A ‘play’ on the Race Cards concept, and inspired by the iconic Grace Jones, this celebratory performance explores the intersectionality of being a Black woman in the 21st century, whilst highlighting the exquisiteness of Black female and non-binary existence.<br>Combining poems, performance, and pole art — *The Grace Card* invites you into a swirling series of questions about our divinity.         
         
Content Warning: please click [here](/warnings).         
         
Chanje is a poet, performance artist, and filmmaker whose work explores 21st-century life — using art to transform lives, shift consciousness, and bring joy to an otherwise serious and stressful modern existence. She creates work across literature, theatre, Live Art, and film, and has presented nationally and internationally, including at the Southbank Centre, the National Arts Festival of South Africa, and Harare International Festival of Arts.         
         
<a href="https://www.chanjekunda.com" target="_blank">chanjekunda.com</a> · Insta <a href="https://instagram.com/chanje.kunda" target="_blank">@chanje.kunda</a> <a href="https://instagram.com/spinning.constellations" target="_blank">@spinning.constellations</a>

**Rachel Smith** · *Self Help with Mother Dark*         
         
Apparently rescuing is ‘enabling’ and ‘helping’ is the sunny side of control, but how do we let people go when there’s nothing left to catch them?<br>*MOTHER DARK* is a blackly comic, cathartic ride through co-dependence, mother rage, and recovery. If you love a self-help group and a banging 90s tune, if you yearn in these times for a (dark) Mother, this show is for you.         
         
Content Warning: please click [here](/warnings).         
         
Rachel is a writer-performer based in Todmorden. An actor turned screenwriter, *MOTHER DARK* marks her return to performance and is her first solo show.         
         
<a href="https://rachelsmithwriterperformer.com" target="_blank">rachelsmithwriterperformer.com</a> · Insta <a href="https://instagram.com/rachthesmith" target="_blank">@rachthesmith</a>

**Erick Santos** · *AutoEscavo (Self-Excavation)*         

What can a Black body do in a state of performance?<br>In this new twenty-minute experiment arising from *AutoEscavo*, Brazilian performer Erick Santos brings together movement, voice, song, and fragments of Portuguese to explore ancestral memory and the body as a place of transformation.<br>Developed during his research stay in Manchester, this intimate solo invites audiences to listen beyond language and discover how gestures, rhythms, and memories can speak across cultural boundaries.         
         
Content Warning: please click [here](/warnings).<br>Access: spoken word is all Portuguese.         
         
Erick is an Afro-Brazilian performer and artist-researcher, currently based in Manchester as a visiting doctoral researcher at Manchester Metropolitan University. His practice brings together physical theatre, dance, voice, and ancestral memory. Since 2019, he has developed and performed *AutoEscavo (Self-Excavation)* — exploring the relationship between Black embodied experience and artistic creation.         
         
<a href="https://ericksantos.pb.online" target="_blank">ericksantos.pb.online</a> · Insta <a href="https://instagram.com/erickperformer" target="_blank">@erickperformer</a>

Conjuring up the unique atmosphere and sociality of greenroom's bar, and the artists and audiences who frequented it. A requiem and a celebration.

**Katherine Lee** · *Tin Ling Ling Dei Ling Ling*         
         
An experimental sound work exploring the tension between beliefs and superstitions, inspired by the ghost myth of Hong Kong’s Nam Koo Terrace during WWII.<br>Using unconventional sound objects closely tied to ritual practice — including rice and singing bowls, alongside live guzheng, human voice, movement, and electronics — Katherine evokes an immersive Asian ritualistic atmosphere.<br>The piece invites us to reflect on how deeply held beliefs can transform into superstition, and why they are internalised even when they cannot be rationally explained.         
         
Commissioned by Brighter Sound and ESEA Contemporary.         
         
Katherine is a Hong Kong-born composer-performer based in the UK, specialising in electronic and experimental music. Her work explores subtle sonic possibilities in instruments, objects, and space — often combining Asian traditions with contemporary Western practices. Many of her projects are site-specific, engaging with spatial dynamics and audience placement to create immersive experiences that invite reflection on sound and perception.         
        
<a href="https://llkatherineleelll.wixsite.com/experimentalcomposer" target="_blank">website</a> · Insta <a href="https://instagram.com/llkatherinelll" target="_blank">@llkatherinelll</a>

**Edy Hurst**         
         
Celebrated comedian Edy Hurst (★★★★★ "*Effortlessly Funny*" — NDSreview) is so glad you're here. And you're here. And you are too. But talk is cheap, and in this economy, we need action. The handbrake is down, and the engine of Cheers is going chugga chugga.<br>Take your seat and prepare for a unique act of gratitude from a man who has really taken on too many hobbies to not share them. Will everyone be uniquely gratted before time is up?<br>What does it mean to say thank you? Choo choo!         
         
Content Warning: please click [here](/warnings).         
         
Edy is a Manchester-based comedian and theatre-maker. As part of Lowry's Developed With cohort, he created *Edy Hurst's Wonderfull Discoverie of Witches in the Countie of Himself*, a show about the Lancashire Witch Trials, the Vengaboys, and ‘nothing else’ — which toured the UK and received five-star reviews at the Edinburgh Fringe. He has been nominated for the BBC New Comedy Award and Best Alternative Act at the North West Comedy Awards, and was a finalist in the Musical Comedy Awards.
