*A [Social Experiment](/socialexperiment) on 19 November, presented by* Word of Warning + Contact         
         
**Social Experiment** is a free and informal, semi-regular evening gathering at Contact for anyone interested in Live Art and contemporary performance to meet, chat, and try ideas out…         
        
If you're a North West artist — this is your <a href="https://socialexperiment.posthaven.com" target="_blank">chance to test out</a> a short performance, intervention, or durational work, in front of an informal audience.         
         
If you're an audience member looking for something a little bit different — this is your chance to take a few risks & meet some artists in our social experiment.

**PHØBIA** · *Untitled (Face Yourself)*        
         
How many faces can one person have?<br>In *Face Yourself*, genderless drag creature PHØBIA creates a face, performs in it, photographs it, wipes it away, and begins again. Across three hours, an archive of discarded identities slowly accumulates. Each photograph preserves a version of the performer that no longer exists.<br>Part endurance performance, part live self-portrait, *Face Yourself* invites audiences to witness the labour, vulnerability, and endless possibilities of transformation.<br>Come and go as you please. The face you leave behind might not be the one you return to.         
         
Access: a durational performance, running throughout the evening; come and go.        
         
PHØBIA is a Manchester-based genderless drag creature and performance artist working across horror drag, autobiographical theatre, and durational Live Art. A Factory International Fellow, their practice combines transformation, endurance, absurdity and vulnerability — challenging expectations of what drag can be. Their work includes *I Am Sixteen and Stupid*, a four-hour durational performance premiered at Emergency 26 for Word of Warning + Contact.         
         
Insta <a href="https://instagram.com/thatcreaturephobia" target="_blank">@thatcreaturephobia</a>

**Nathan Birkinshaw** · *How To Be Content*         
         
*"Making peace with the fact this might be it."*         
         
An exploration of contentment, led by a man brimming with mediocrity.         
         
Through film, performance, song, and an increasingly questionable process, comes a show that investigates how to find meaning in the mundane — that somehow becomes increasingly more performative itself as time slips by.
